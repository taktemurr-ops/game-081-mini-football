# Game-081 — Release gates

This source tree is a Flutter **prototype**, not a finished commercial football game.

## Included
- Offline five-round penalty shootout
- Aim for left/center/right; opponent goalkeeper and shooter AI
- Goals, saves, automatic early winner determination, replay
- Six simulation unit tests
- CI definition: Flutter analyze, test, Android debug APK

## Missing before final release
- Verify CI results, fix analysis/test failures, test on physical Android and iOS devices
- Authentic player animation, stadium audio, art, accessibility, languages
- More match formats, leagues, difficulty modes and progression
- Reliable persisted progress and consent-aware privacy flows
- Real production advertising network and policy review
- Store billing with receipt validation and restore/refund handling
- Signed Android App Bundle and iOS IPA, screenshots, listings, privacy disclosures
- End-to-end device QA and App Store / Google Play approval

No claim of production readiness until all gates have evidence.
